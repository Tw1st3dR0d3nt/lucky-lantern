DISCORD BOT

Bring a little luck to your server.

Lucky Lantern is a lightweight Discord bot focused on games, random choices, and server killboards — built to keep things simple and fun.

Add to Discord View on GitHub

Replace YOUR_CLIENT_ID with Lucky Lantern's Discord application ID.

FEATURES

Simple commands. Endless possibilities.

Entertainment and utility slash commands without unnecessary access to your server.

🎱

Eight Ball

Ask a yes-or-no question and let the mysterious Eight Ball decide your fate.

🎲

Dice & Coin Flip

Roll configurable dice or settle a decision with a quick virtual coin flip.

🎡

Roulette

Spin the wheel and see where the ball lands.

🔮

Random Choice

Give Lucky Lantern multiple options and let it randomly pick one.

🩸

Killboard

Track server-defined killboard entries and view the leaderboard.

🛡️

Server Controls

Authorized administrators can edit, remove, or clear killboard entries.

PRIVACY-FIRST DESIGN

Only the Discord information Lucky Lantern needs.

Lucky Lantern uses Discord's Guilds intent and processes information required to respond to commands and maintain its features. It does not require broad message-content access to operate.
